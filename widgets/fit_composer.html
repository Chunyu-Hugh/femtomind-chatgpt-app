What this is. Each experiment comes as a dataset of measured points. The cuts column is the kinematic selection applied before fitting — they drop the nucleon resonance region (W² < 10 GeV²) and the low-Q² region where higher-twist effects matter. These are the standard JAM cuts; change them only with a reason.
PDF Parameters
What this is. Every parton flavor is modelled by the template f(x) = N·xa(1−x)b(1 + c√x + dx). N is fixed by the valence and momentum sum rules for uv, dv and the gluon; the sea starts symmetric (S₁ = S₂, δ = 0) and is split flavor by flavor as the ladder adds discriminating data. Value is the starting point, [Min, Max] the fit bounds, fixed pins a parameter to its value.

Job Settings
What this is. The perturbative framework the fit runs in. The defaults reproduce the JAM global-analysis setup: NLO accuracy, truncated DGLAP evolution in the zero-mass variable-flavor scheme, and the input scale Q₀² = mc² = 1.28² GeV². Target-mass and higher-twist corrections are left off — the kinematic cuts in the previous step handle the region where they matter.
