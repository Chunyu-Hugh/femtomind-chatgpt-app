What the smoke established about the instrument: the sandbox holds (all 18
attack checks blocked, `attack`), the token-mode gateway with the server-side
deny list serves 22 tools and none of the hidden ones (`preflight`), the
bridge and sign-in work in the box (`calibrate`, $0.02–0.03 a run), the
classifier reproduces the launcher's numbers, the probe finds and cancels only
the run's own job, the fetch follows a job script's variables, `cd` and loop
globs to the inputs it ran, the seven-step chain loads and is graded as
FitPack would load it, and the aggregate table comes out with intervals.

Two things it taught: FitPack's stdout is often redirected by the agent, so
fit evidence must be looked for inside the run directory (done); and the
no-MCP agent again hit the partition's time limit on its second submission,
as the pilot's run3 did.
